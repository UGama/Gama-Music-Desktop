Gama Music v27 replacement files

Replace these files in your project:
- web/index.html
- web/service-worker.js
- web/styles.css
- web/assets/icon.svg
- web/assets/icon-192.png
- web/assets/icon-512.png
- web/assets/apple-touch-icon.png

app.js is included unchanged for convenience.

After replacing:
1. Restart the Gama Music server.
2. In Safari refresh the page.
3. For the Home Screen icon, remove the old Home Screen Gama Music icon and add it again from Safari.
   Note: removing the installed web app may clear iPhone-only IndexedDB offline copies.
